### 🏠 ak-FAMILY (Family OS) — *Family Digital Management Platform*

A modular, feature-based family management platform being built end-to-end — health, finance, education, documents, places, permissions, alerts, and more, each with its own dedicated analytics dashboard.

**Highlights:**
- 🧩 10 feature modules: Health & Emergency, Finance, Education, Documents, Places, Permissions, Alerts, Family, Auth, Settings
- 📊 "Family Command Center" dashboard with live analytics per module (Recharts)
- 🚨 Health Intelligence Engine — Family Health Score, Smart Insights, Emergency Readiness %
- 📍 Live Location Tracking with real road routing (OSRM) and free geocoding (Nominatim)
- 🆘 Emergency Mode — live location share + WhatsApp emergency broadcast
- 💰 Centralized precision finance engine to keep every number consistent across the app

`React` • `Vite` • `Tailwind CSS` • `Zustand` • `Recharts` • `react-router-dom`
